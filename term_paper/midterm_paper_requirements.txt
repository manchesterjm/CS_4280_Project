Midterm Report Guidelines

Your Midterm Report builds directly on your Proposal Report and must include these sections:

    Title, Authors, Abstract, and Introduction (revised as needed)

    Related Work: Expand with the three new papers you read.

    Methodology: Describe the algorithm(s), data, or systems you implemented.

    Experiments and Results: Discuss your design, testing plan, and any obtained results.

    Conclusion: Summarize progress, findings, and remaining work.

    You may not include any figures or images taken from external sources. All visuals must be original (e.g., recreated or generated by you).

Submission

    Submit both your Midterm Report (AAAI format PDF) and presentation slides to Canvas before your presentation day.

    The report should reflect your individual contribution, even if you are in a team.

    Each team should ensure their content aligns, but each student must present their own slides and results.

Evaluation

    Midterm presentations and its associated report account for 12.5% of your final course grade (see syllabus).

    Grading will be based on clarity, technical depth, progress since proposal, and completeness of your report which also include clearity and quality of your writing.

Please note, no late submissions will be allowed.
