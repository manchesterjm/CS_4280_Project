Presentation Schedule

Your final presentations will take place during the last week of class (12/09/2025 & 12/11/2025).
Each student will have 6 minutes total — please plan your time carefully.

Time Breakdown:

    5 minutes for presentation including 20s final demo (pre-recorded video)

    1 minute for Q&A and transition

Because our time is very limited, we must keep a strict schedule. Please be fully prepared and move quickly between teams.

If a student or team is not ready at their assigned time, we will move to the next presenter. There will be no extra time or rescheduling.
Submission Requirements

All materials must be uploaded to Canvas by December 18, 2025 (11:59 PM).
This gives everyone an extra week after presentations to finalize reports, fix issues raised during presentations, and polish their submissions.

Each student must submit:

    Final Report (AAAI format, PDF)

    Project Code (with clear instructions on how to run it)

    Presentation Slides

    Demo Video (~20 seconds) showing your implementation or results

Report Content & Expectations

Your final report builds on your midterm version and should demonstrate progress, refinement, and completion.
Use your midterm report as the foundation and update or expand the following sections:

    Related Work: Add at least three new references (beyond your midterm).

    Method: Update based on your final implementation.

    Results & Discussion: Include your final experimental results, comparisons against state of the art and if possible, against your team-mates' results, and analysis.

    Conclusion: Summarize your project’s findings, significance, and potential future work.

    You likely need to also revise your Abstract and Introduction for clarity and completeness.

Additional Guidelines

    Use only original figures and visuals. Recreate any charts or diagrams you refer to.

    Clearly describe your dataset, parameters, and algorithms.

    Ensure your code is executable and easy to test using your provided instructions.

    Each student in a team must show their own demo clip (20 seconds) and contribution in their slides.

Evaluation

Your Final Presentation + Report together account for 25% of your total course grade.
Grading will emphasize:

    Technical completeness and progress since midterm

    Clarity and quality of writing

    Quality of code and demonstration

    Integration of feedback from previous stages
