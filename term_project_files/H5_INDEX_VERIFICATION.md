## What is H5 Index?

**Definition**: The h5-index is the h-index for articles published in the last 5 complete years. It is the largest number h such that h articles published in 2020-2024 have at least h citations each.

**Example**: Nature Communications (H5=399) means it has 399 articles from 2020-2024 that each have at least 399 citations.

**Source**: Google Scholar Metrics (scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=top_venues)
**Data Period**: 2020-2024
**Last Updated**: July 2025
